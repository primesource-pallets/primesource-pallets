## The data side

The business runs on tracked numbers: every pallet processed gets logged —
purchase cost, delivery share, testing time, listing time, sale price, fees, net.
Twelve months of that data across ~9,000 line items produced the guides above
(average net £246/pallet; top 20% of items = 71% of profit; testing worth a 2.9x
price multiplier). Spreadsheets &gt; opinions.
